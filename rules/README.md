# rules

Clinical and eligibility rules, written as data rather than buried in code.

**You do not need to read any code to review these.** That is the entire point. A clinician
should be able to open a rule, see what it says, disagree with it, and propose a change.

Every rule records:

- what it says
- the clinical guideline or source it comes from
- its version, and when it was last checked
- who reviewed it, and when

If a rule here is wrong, outdated, or does not reflect Indian clinical practice, please tell us.
That is one of the most valuable contributions anyone can make to this project, and it needs no
technical skill at all.

**These are not clinical guidance.** They are our working encoding of published guidance, for
simulation purposes, and they may contain errors.
